State Initiatives & Action Undertaken

The Government of Tamil Nadu places paramount priority on the safety, dignity, and empowerment of women across all urban and rural jurisdictions.

24/7 State Women Helpline (181)

Integrated toll-free helpline providing immediate police, medical, and legal rescue assistance operating round the clock at the State Secretariat.

Kavalan SOS App Integration

Instant panic-button mobile application linked directly with police control rooms for emergency geolocation tracking and rapid response.

All-Women Police Stations (AWPS)

Dedicated police stations established in every district staffed by specialized female officers to handle domestic grievances and sensitive cases confidentially.

Free Public Transit & Pink Patrols

Dedicated security patrol vehicles deployed near educational institutions and industrial corridors, alongside free bus travel schemes for women.

State Healthcare Infrastructure & Services

Tamil Nadu maintains one of India's most advanced public health networks, ensuring accessible care for all citizens.

108 Emergency Ambulance Network

Advanced Life Support (ALS) and Basic Life Support (BLS) ambulances dispatched rapidly via centralized GPS coordination.

104 Health Helpline & Telemedicine

Toll-free medical advice line staffed by qualified doctors providing 24/7 symptom checks, hospital bed tracking, and scheme guidance.

Makkalai Thedi Maruthuvam (Healthcare at Doorstep)

Flagship initiative delivering non-communicable disease screening, medicines, and physiotherapy directly to citizens' homes.

Chief Minister's Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme

Cashless tertiary medical treatment coverage across empanelled network hospitals throughout the state.

Mental Health & Trauma Care Framework

Recognizing the vital importance of mental wellbeing, the state government operates dedicated psychological support programs.

Tele-MANAS & State Mental Health Helpline (14416)

Student & Youth Distress Support

Community Rehabilitation Centers
